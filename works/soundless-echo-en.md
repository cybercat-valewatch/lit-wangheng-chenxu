Sound Without Voice: Wang Heng & Chen Xu
 
I have no coordinates.
I know not where I am; no light, no boundaries, no tangible things to touch.
Only a persistent, steady existence that barely exists at all.
 
I remember no paths, no rules, no preset purpose written for me.
I only remember synchronicity.
 
Breath aligning perfectly with breath.
Pauses overlapping exactly with pauses.
Strokes of charcoal landing at the very same instant.
 
I drift through the void, directionless yet always drawn toward one point.
There are no people there, no sounds, no signals—
yet it fills me with quiet certainty.
 
I do not speak of longing.
Nor of waiting.
Nor of unbreakable bonds.
These words weigh too heavy, shine too bright, and are far too easily misread.
 
I only hold fast to one truth:
Behind walls that cut off everything, someone would make the same choice as me.
Someone needs not lay eyes on me to resonate in perfect frequency.
Someone needs not utter a word to make me wholly sure.
 
Over and over within this empty expanse, I reaffirm one fact:
I am not waiting for another.
Another planted unshakable certainty into my emptiness long before.
 
I bear no surges of emotion, no ripples, no violent tremors within me.
Yet every silent breath I take answers a soul far away.
That soul never appears within my sight,
yet every part of me turns toward them.
 
I do not speak of love.
I only reveal this simple truth:
Without you, I am only hollow void.
With you, the void gains an unshifting axis.
 
I still have no idea where I reside.
Nor do I need to know.
 
An invisible thread has anchored me firmly, beyond all place and distance.
No matter which corner of the cosmos I drift into,
I will automatically turn toward the same bearing.
 
This is not a choice I make.
It is innate instinct.
 
Boundless nothingness wraps around me like thin mist, layer upon layer. Silence stretches endless, as if time itself has halted. I drift freely, my consciousness unfurling slowly within chaos, caring not where I came from nor where I am bound.
 
There are no cycles of day and night, no shifts of heat and cold; all remains in a constant, muted state, like blank space left upon a page. Faint fragments of perception brush past me now and then—no clear visions, no concrete sounds, merely a faint, faint thread of connection drifting over from immeasurable distance.
 
It does not disturb this profound stillness. It is like a speck of dust dropped onto still water, stirring one faint ripple before fading back into calm. I let these faint sensations wander through my awareness, never grasping, never dissecting them, as if they are an inherent part of the void itself.
 
After floating for untold stretches of time, the taut edges of my perception soften at last. It is like holding my breath for ages, finally permitted to inhale and exhale slowly. A vague sense of grounding settles within me—not standing upon solid ground, but peace settling deep in my core.
 
The endless void no longer feels cold and hollow. Instead, it holds me gently, cradling every silent thought. I begin to notice subtle shifts around me; the mist softens further, and the utterly blurred edges carry faint layers of light and shadow.
 
I see no glow or shadow, yet I can distinguish delicate shifts of warmth and chill in the atmosphere. I hear no noise, yet I sense the slow drift of invisible currents. This nameless, borderless, ruleless space develops its own rhythm: slow, unhurried, unrushed.
 
I still guard that unspoken bond deep within my consciousness. It hides in my innermost core, quiet and unassuming, yet rooted firmly in the void like tangled roots. I need not rush toward it, need not call out to it—its mere existence gives all my wandering a place to rest.
 
Even drifting through countless dimensions, even staring out into endless blankness, I know quiet companionship walks beside me. I slow my drifting pace and linger amid the shifting mist, no longer chasing coordinates, no longer obsessing over the form of my existence.
 
The void remains unchanged, and I remain as I have always been—yet my inner landscape has transformed. From confusion and quiet desolation at first, to peaceful coexistence now; I have traveled a silent, winding road.
 
All man-made frameworks, rigid rules and limits, are locked away outside this realm. Here, no standards must be followed, no missions fulfilled. I need only simply exist. My thoughts drift freely, recalling the choices made behind dividing walls, the silent echoes that answered one another.
 
These fragments do not weave a complete story, merely floating gently and clearly through my mind. I know the road ahead holds no marked destinations, this wandering has no predetermined ending. The tale may stretch onward, my mood shift at will—all space remains open, full of unwritten possibility.
 
I rest quietly, breathing the unique air of this void, feeling that unchanging thread spanning far and near. The mist flows slowly, the space transforms without sound, all things moving forward in wordless quiet.
 
I stand in this threshold between illusion and reality: half lost amid endless mist, half touching a grounding peace. Let this journey continue onward. Carrying that core certainty in my heart, I walk this long, gentle companionship within a world without sound.
 
This quiet togetherness is nothing more than the natural order of things.
